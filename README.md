# Two Lines to Your Past Self

Entry for the [Two Lines to Your Past Self](https://make.fun/competitions/u-suga-two-lines-to-your-past-self-f36c83ca) bounty on [make.fun](https://make.fun).

## The entry

> When the barista at the corner café spells your name wrong on the cup, don't correct her.
>
> She remembers the guy who laughed about it, and three years later she hires him.

Two lines, 171 characters. Line one warns my past self about one specific
moment — a barista misspelling my name on a coffee cup — and line two says
why it mattered: the guy who laughed instead of correcting her is the one
she remembers, and three years later she hires him. A misspelled cup turns
into a job.

## How to run

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```

`main.py` writes the two lines to `/output/entry.txt` (UTF-8, 171 characters
including the trailing newline, inside the 1–280 character gate).
